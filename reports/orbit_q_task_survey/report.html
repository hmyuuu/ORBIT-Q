Prioritized roadmap · Survey inference
  • 1. Add rolling or private tasks and stronger runtime provenance.
  • 2. Use blinded or multi-auditor semantic review.

  • 4. Broaden task families, including QEC simulation and decoding.
  • 5. Record gradient and memory-complexity diagnostics for scalability claims.
